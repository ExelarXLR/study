## Retrieval policy

Strong concepts are not removed from study. They return at longer intervals.

Suggested adaptive pattern:

- same session / next session
- ~2 days
- ~1 week
- ~2-3 weeks
- later cumulative review

If retrieval fails, shorten the interval and diagnose whether the failure is conceptual or memory-based.
